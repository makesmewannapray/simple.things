ScanToPDF

Turn paper piles and loose images into one tidy PDF – and fix every page before you save.
Works fully offline, so your documents never leave your computer.

Download for Windows macOS – coming soon

01 — A quick look

02 — What it does

  • Scans straight from any TWAIN-compatible scanner.
  • No scanner? Drop in photos, images, or PDFs you already have.
  • Rotate, delete, and reorder pages before you save.
  • Merges several files into one tidy PDF.
  • Works fully offline – your files never leave your computer.

03 — Good to know

ScanToPDF is free and always will be. It uses only what Windows already has, keeps nothing about you, and never goes online your files stay yours. A macOS version is on the way.

Like every tool here, it started as a small annoyance in my own routine. It comes as it is, with no warranty – just a small tool, shared in good faith.
